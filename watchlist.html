What's in a Watchlist?

I believe curating a vibe and making a goal makes content less mindless when you are consuming it. With this belief in mind, here are the movies and shows I want to watch this fall. I'm really excited for this one! It also marks the last quarter of 2025, so we gotta make it count!

Movies To Watch

  1. Him – I'm a longtime fan of Jordan Peele. He produced this movie and anything with a Peele stamp of approval will have a watch from me.
  2. Bones and All - I am so late to this, but have been hankering to watch it ever since I saw a Taylor Russell inteview on Tiktok. It also feels like a fall movie.
  3. Silent Hill - A horror classic! I like the video game, I feel like aesthetically, this movie is going to impress me. It's hard for me to actually watch scary movies but I will try my best.

Shows To Watch

  • Succession – I’m currently watching this. On episode 4 and I love it. Trying to watch an episode as I eat dinner, but it is hard to pay attention and eat at the same time.
  • Arrested Development- I always see funny clips of this show but never watched a full episode. It feels like a nice, light, sitcom I can watch during meals.
