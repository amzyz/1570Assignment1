Best Childhood Movies

Movies I watched as a kid that formed the jellies of my brain. Here they are:

  • Stick It
  • The Pursuit of Happyness
  • Akeelah and the Bee

Featured Childhood Movie

Stick It remains in my brain as the best gymnastics movie of all time. It has everything: a rebellious main character who reads lesbian, a redemption arc (!) and feminist leanings!!

Best Comedy Movies

I think I'm a funny person. Here are movies I think are really really funny:

  • The Spy Who Dumped Me
  • Ocean's 8
  • Pitch Perfect

Featured Comedy Movie

The Spy Who Dumped Me was a revelation. Seriously, Kate McKinnon and Mila Kunis together was PERFECTION. I watched this in theaters with my mother and we were both laughing OUT LOUD.

Best Sad Movies

Last year, I watched a bunch of sad movies in the dead of winter. Most were critically acclaimed from a year or two ago that I had missed. I was also grieving then, so it was nice to cry over something other than my own circumstances.

If you hate really sad movies, click this button.

  • Past Lives
  • Moonlight
  • Aftersun
  • All Of Us Strangers

Featured Sad Movie

Aftersun was moody, quiet, somber, some may say boring. I sat there transfixed as I watched it in my dark room alone. It was beautiful. I would watch it again if I was going through something terrible hahah.

Best Joy-Inducing Movies

Sometimes, a good movie will save you. Here are movies I watch if I'm down that remind me that there is JOY in the world!!

  1. Top Gun: Maverick
  2. Pitch Perfect (ALL)
  3. Fantastic Mr. Fox
  4. Bottoms

Featured Joyous Movie

Top Gun: Maverick is probably my favorite movie of the 2020s and I'm not even being ironic. It is cheesy, wholesome, and directed and produced by Tom Cruise so of course it's got that all-American flavor. I love it so much.
